01 — What it is

What is Open Competition?

Open Competition refers to the research grants the Foundation awards through open calls. Anyone in the research community who meets a call's eligibility criteria may apply, and applications are assessed by independent expert committees.

It is one of several ways the Foundation distributes funding. The table sets it apart from the others, so you know what can — and cannot — be applied for here.

05 — Applying

How to apply

All calls and deadlines are announced on the calls page. Applications are prepared and submitted via the Foundation's online system, NORMA, before each call's deadline. Every call has its own guidelines — read them carefully first.

  1. 1Find the call that fits your project's aim and idea.
  2. 2Read the guidelines and check you are eligible.
  3. 3Log in to NORMA in good time and get familiar with it.
  4. 4Coordinate with your host institution (hosting letter, salary).
  5. 5Complete all fields as described, proofread, and submit early.

How applications are assessed

After the deadline, the secretariat sends your application to an independent expert committee of 5–12 members. They assess quality, novelty and impact, the budget, and the applicant's qualifications.

06 — Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which areas can I apply within?

A broad field — from biomedicine and health science, education & outreach, biotechnology and natural & technical sciences, to art research.

Who assesses my application?

Committees of Danish and international experts. Some calls also use internal committees and external peer review, as stated in the guidelines.

Will I get feedback on my application?

No. The Foundation does not have the capacity to provide individual feedback on all applications.

How many applications are funded?

On average around 20% across the Foundation, varying by area, grant type and competition.

Is diversity part of the assessment?

Yes. The Foundation follows a diversity policy for governance, committee composition and the assessment of applications.

Can I see who has been funded?

Yes. All grants — with applicant, recipient, location, title, amount and year — appear in the grant database.

Does the Foundation cover overhead?

No. You may allocate up to 5% of the budget to direct administrative costs, included within the total budget.

Do I have to report if I am funded?

Yes. Activities and results are tracked via digital reporting systems (researchfish® and FoundGood); the scope depends on the grant type.
